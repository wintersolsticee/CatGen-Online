# 🐾 CatGen Online

**Live the life of a warrior cat.** CatGen Online is a free multiplayer game you play right in
your web browser. There's nothing to download or install.

Create your own cat, join one of the five Clans, and explore a living forest with other players.
You can hunt prey, gather healing herbs, defend your borders, rise through the ranks, and meet in
peace at Fourtrees beneath the full moon.

## ▶️ Play now

**👉 [playcatgen.gg](https://playcatgen.gg)**

Works on any modern desktop browser (Chrome, Edge, Firefox or Safari).

## 📝 How to join

1. Go to **[playcatgen.gg](https://playcatgen.gg)** and click **Join the Clans**.
2. **Create your account.** Pick a username and a password (at least 8 characters).
3. **Create your cat.**
   - Give your cat a name.
   - Choose a Clan: **ThunderClan**, **RiverClan**, **WindClan**, **ShadowClan** or **SkyClan**.
     Or live as a **loner**.
   - Choose your starting rank: kit, apprentice, medicine cat apprentice or warrior.
   - Pick your cat's look, coat shade and size.
4. Click **Enter the forest**, then **Play**. You'll wake up in your Clan's camp!

Each account can have up to **5 cats**, and your cats are saved automatically.

## 🌲 The world

- **Five Clan territories** with their own camps, dens, fresh-kill pile and medicine den.
- **Forests, moorland, pine woods, marshes, a river, a lake, cliffs and a hidden cave.**
- **Day and night, four seasons** (Newleaf, Greenleaf, Leaf-fall and Leaf-bare) and changing
  weather: rain, storms, fog and snow.
- **Landmarks to discover**, including Fourtrees, Sunningrocks, Snakerocks and the Moonstone Cave.

## 🐭 What you can do

- **Hunt.** Prey notices you, so stalk low through the grass and pounce! Carry your catch home
  to the fresh-kill pile to feed your Clan.
- **Heal.** Gather herbs and learn what each one cures. Medicine cats keep their Clan alive
  through wounds, infections, fevers and greencough.
- **Fight.** Swipe, bite, pounce, block and dodge. Battles leave injuries that need treatment.
- **Rise through the ranks.** Go from kit to apprentice to warrior and beyond, through
  ceremonies held by your leader and deputy.
- **Make friends.** Chat nearby, with your Clan or with everyone, whisper, use emotes and add
  friends.
- **Gather.** At every full moon the Clans meet at Fourtrees under a truce.

## 🎮 Controls

| Key | Action |
| --- | --- |
| **W A S D** or arrow keys | Move |
| **Shift** | Run |
| **C** | Stalk (sneak up on prey) |
| **E** | Interact: gather herbs, drop prey, eat |
| **1 / 2 / 3** | Swipe / Bite / Pounce (aim with your mouse) |
| **Q** / **Space** | Block / Dodge |
| **Enter** | Chat |
| **Click a cat** | See their profile, whisper, add friend, treat injuries |
| **I / K / O / M** | Inventory / Clan / Friends / Map |
| **Mouse wheel** | Zoom |

## 🤝 Community rules
